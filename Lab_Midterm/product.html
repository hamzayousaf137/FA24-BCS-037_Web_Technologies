Headphones

Rs. 2,500




Description

Comfortable wireless headphones with clear sound and long battery life.

These headphones use soft memory-foam ear cushions. You can wear them for many hours without feeling pressure or heat. The headband is padded and adjusts to fit any head.

The sound is clear and balanced. Bass is deep but never overpowers the vocals, and the highs stay crisp even at high volume. They work well for music, movies, online classes and calls.

The battery lasts up to 30 hours on one charge. A fifteen minute quick charge gives about three extra hours.

Bluetooth connects in seconds and stays stable up to ten metres. You can also use the included cable with any device that has a headphone jack.

A built-in microphone with noise reduction keeps your voice clear on calls. Buttons on the ear cup control volume, tracks and calls.

Every pair comes with a hard carrying case, a charging cable, a one year warranty and a seven day return policy.
